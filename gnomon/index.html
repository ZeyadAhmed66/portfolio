01 / A CLEARER WORKING DAY

Less chasing updates.
More knowing who’s here.

“Signed in.” “Taking a break.” “On annual leave.” When those updates lived in email, it was hard to see who was available without piecing the messages together.

I built Gnomon for Pollion to give that working day a shared home: team availability, attendance and time off, with a personal view of leave balances.

04 / CLARITY FOR EACH EMPLOYEE

Your time.
In your own view.

Gnomon also gives employees a place to check their annual leave balance, review their time off and make requests. Managers can review requests, while the team gets visibility into who is away.

Attendance, breaks and leave belong to the same working day. Bringing them together makes the app useful to the people recording time and the people coordinating the team.
